Direction 1 · turn-taking

1 · The tide

Khao soi · chicken · mild
Tap for what it is

Idea: the bottom third of the screen is a wash that belongs to whoever should speak. When it's the vendor's turn, the tide rises in clay and says ตาคุณ พูดได้เลย in huge Thai. Readable from arm's length, no reading required.

Pushes the core rule (color = who speaks) from the bubble to the whole screen. Directly attacks R2: the vendor doesn't reply because they don't know it's their turn.

Cost: S, one div + a CSS transition keyed on turn state

Idea: kill the chat log. Only the current exchange exists, set like a poster: answers at 34px, numbered to match your questions. History shrinks to a ribbon of chips at the top.

Answers 1-2-3 mirror questions 1-2-3: you see instantly which question got which answer. Huge from the back of the room on the demo screen.

Idea: the logo's bamboo weave becomes the spine of the conversation. Each message is a knot, indigo or clay. The context card, which belongs to both, is woven from both colors.

The story in one image: two threads becoming one fabric. Great for slides and the opening shot. Weaker on legibility: bubbles are half-width.

Cost: S for the card border only, M for the full spine

Idea: the context card is an object, a torn market ticket with a Thai name printed big and an ink stamp. The stamp says who vouched ("said by vendor"): honest about R4 without a warning color.

The slip is also what you would screenshot and show at the next stall, or keep as a Memory (V2). An object people want to collect.

Cost: S, pure CSS on the existing card

5 · Read-along

  • จานนี้คืออะไรครับ

  • ผมแพ้ถั่วลิสง มีถั่วลิสงไหมครับ
What is it? Is it spicy? I'm allergic to peanuts: any in it?
ตาคุณ กดไมค์แล้วพูดได้เลย
ไทย
English

Idea: while the Thai is spoken aloud, each line lights up in sync, karaoke-style, and the waveform fills. The vendor follows with their eyes and ears at once, then the hint hands them the mic.

Shown on screen 4 here (it's the moment it matters), but it applies to every bubble. Makes the "designed for the other side" argument visible in 3 seconds.

Cost: S, speechSynthesis gives an onend per utterance: speak line by line
