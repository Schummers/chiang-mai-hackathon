Two voices,
one paper.

Color means one thing only: who is speaking. Indigo washes hold everything the visitor says, clay washes hold everything the vendor says, and the ink stays the same for both so every line reads at full contrast in market sunlight. State (safe, spicy, allergy) never gets a color; it gets weight, shape and position.

What's new vs the rejected directions.html

8 colors per direction down to 7 totalNo success green, no danger red, no header fills. Near-white paper, one ink.
Color is a speaker, not a stateClay no longer marks buttons, spice or Thai text. It marks the vendor, and only as a background wash.
Allergy by inversionA conflict flips to a solid ink stamp on top of the card. The darkest thing on screen, never red.
Built for sunlightAtkinson Hyperlegible for Latin, looped Thai for vendors used to printed signage, 2.1px icon strokes.

Palette, 7 colors

Secondary text and hairlines are ink at 70% and 10% alpha, not extra colors. Clay is pushed toward brown (hue 18, low chroma) so it reads as earth and wood, never as a warning.

Typography

Two families, both chosen for reading at arm's length in glare. Thai is always set one step larger than the Latin next to it, because Thai glyphs sit smaller on the same size.

Card levels and background layers

Only two backgrounds exist under content: paper (the page) and white (lifted). Washes sit flat on paper, they carry identity, not depth. Depth is reserved for things with information you act on.

L0 · GroundPaper, no border, no shadow. Screen background, dock.bg paper
L1 · Voice (you)Flat wash, radius 18, tail corner 6. Messages.bg you-wash · no shadow
