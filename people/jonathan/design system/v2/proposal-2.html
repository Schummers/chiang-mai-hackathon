Dark thread,
light thread.

In Chiang Mai noon sun, hue washes out but light and dark survive. So the two voices are told apart by brightness, like the two threads of the logo's weave: the visitor speaks in solid indigo slabs with white text, the vendor answers on pale sand with graphite text. No shadows anywhere, because shadows vanish in glare and borders do not.

What's new vs proposal 1 (Two voices, one paper)

Luminance, not two washesP1 used two pale tints that look alike in a grayscale photo. Here one voice is dark, one is light: still readable with sunglasses on.
Full-width slabs, no chat bubblesNothing aligns left or right. Every turn spans the screen, so long Thai lines get the full width and nothing reads like a messaging app.
Allergy as textureA hatched band on the dish card, like a strip of woven bamboo, plus a graphite frame. No red, no solid black stamp.
Phone flat on the counterShow mode keeps a small upside-down strip facing you across the stall, so neither person has to lean in or grab.

Palette, 6 colors

Secondary text, hairlines and the allergy hatch are graphite at 74%, 12% and 13% alpha, never extra hues. Indigo is a touch lighter and bluer than the logo's #2B3263 so white text on it keeps 11:1 contrast and the slab still reads as indigo, not black, in shade. Clay is pulled toward dry, unglazed earth (low chroma, hue 25): it never fills a large area, so it cannot read as an alert.

Typography

Two civic faces. Sarabun is the open successor of TH Sarabun PSK, the font of every Thai government form and school paper, so a vendor of any age reads it without effort. Public Sans comes from the US federal design system, built for forms read in a hurry. Thai always sits 2 to 4px larger than the Latin next to it.

Card levels and background layers

Flat on purpose. Depth comes from nesting and borders, never from shadow: L1 slabs sit on the rice ground, L2 white cards sit only inside the vendor's sand slab, L3 is anchored to the screen edge. The dashed indigo outline is a special state: your next turn, not sent yet.
