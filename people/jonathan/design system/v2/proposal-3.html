Their half,
your half.

Every conversation at a stall is a handshake, so every card is split in two: the top half, in banana-leaf wash, is what the vendor said or means; the bottom half, in indigo wash, is what it means for you. The vendor's color moves from clay to banana leaf, the material every Northern dish is wrapped in, so nothing on screen can ever read as red. A state takes the color of whoever it concerns: your pork or peanut conflict is yours, so it turns your half solid indigo.

What's new vs proposal 1 (Two voices, one paper) and 2 (Dark thread, light thread)

Vendor = banana leaf, not clayP1 and P2 fought to keep clay from reading as red. Here clay stays in the logo only. Leaf green is earthy, cool beside indigo, and carries no alarm.
Split cards, not bubbles or slabsA dish card is half theirs, half yours. You read what the dish is on leaf, then what it means for you on indigo, in one glance.
Allergy in your own colorNo stamp, no hatch, no extra hue: a conflict just fills your half with solid indigo. It is the only solid block in the stream.
Learn while you orderThe vendor's reply carries a word-by-word gloss, and show mode offers price buttons (30 to 80 ฿), the reply vendors give most.

Palette, 7 colors

Two identity hues, each as a solid and a wash, plus paper, white and one ink. Secondary text and hairlines are ink at 72% and 10% alpha. Leaf sits at hue 105 with low chroma: it reads as a dried banana leaf, not as a "success" green, and it never marks a state.

Typography

Loopless Thai for the vendor, because it is the style of shop banners, menus and price boards at every Chiang Mai market. A wide, open Latin for the visitor, built to be read fast. Thai always sits 3 to 4px above the Latin next to it.

Icons: Material Symbols Rounded

Material Symbols Rounded 0.47.5 (npm material-symbols, from jsdelivr). One variable font instead of a set of SVGs: weight 500 by default, fill 1 for whichever mic is live, and the grade axis for sunlight. Rounded terminals echo the soft ends of the leaf-cut corners. Icons take ink or the speaker's color, never a state color.

Card levels and background layers

Depth is almost flat. Turns are washes with a leaf cut: the short corner points at the speaker's side. The split card is the one composite object. Only the dock is lifted, so the thumb zone always reads as "on top".
