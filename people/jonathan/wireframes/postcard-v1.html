Postcard v1

The image a Visitor saves to Photos at the end of an exchange. Three directions, each without and with the dish photo (ticket 06), shown at phone size, 9:16 so it also works as a story. Kratip tokens, two fonts, no new color.

Scene: Warorot Market, Sunday 27 September 2026. The Visitor said the Echo ลำขนาดเจ้า (lam kha-nat jao, "Very delicious!", Kham Mueang, echo-lam) over a khao soi. The Vendor answered ยินดีเจ้า กินอีกนะ (built from the likelyReplies of say-very-delicious, to be checked by a Thai teammate). Photo = CSS placeholder, no people.
