Animations

#WhenWhatWhy
1First tap on the micThe language / info setup slides up into the top barThe setup has done its job, the screen belongs to the conversation
2Recording (steps 1, 4)Full screen in the speaker's color, a wave that follows the voice, a timer"I'm listening", impossible to miss, even for the vendor
3Processing (step 2)A light runs around the raw-transcript bubble, with "…"Makes the 2 to 5 s wait feel alive (R3)
4Result (step 3)The bubble morphs from raw to final: Thai big, English small, bulletsYou see the rephrasing happen: that's the product's value
5Audio playingSmall equalizer in the bubbleTells both people the phone is speaking
6Whose turnThe mic of whoever should speak next pulsesSolves "the vendor doesn't know where to tap" (R2)
7Context cardSlides in 0.4 s after the replyReads as an extra, not part of what the vendor said

For the tech check

  • Auto-play the Thai (step 3): iOS blocks sound without a tap. The mic tap can unlock audio for the session. To test on an iPhone. Without it, step 3 needs a ▶ tap.
  • Morph raw → final (animation 4): needs the raw transcript first, then the model output. Two steps on the server, not one call.
  • Bullets: the model returns structured output (a list of items per language), not free text.
  • Context card: in the same model response (a field "card" that is empty when no dish or ingredient is named), so no extra wait.
