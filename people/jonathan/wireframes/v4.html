For the tech check

  • Auto-play the Thai (step 4): iOS blocks sound without a tap. The mic tap can unlock audio for the session. To test on an iPhone. Without it, the 🔊 is the fallback.
  • Raw → final (steps 3 to 4): transcription first (shown right away), then one model call.
  • One structured model response: translation items, original items, and an optional context card (empty when no dish or ingredient is named).
  • My info: stored on the phone (no account), sent with every request.
